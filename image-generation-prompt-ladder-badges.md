# Ladder Badge Image-Generation Prompt Set — Level Up Athletics

For use with a third-party image generator (Midjourney, DALL·E, Stable Diffusion, etc.). Built from the project's mood board (creamsicle + neon-blue-led palette, BL101/Overtime streetwear reference, chunky rounded type) and the graffiti-lettering direction from the ladder rename.

**How to use this:** most image generators don't remember prior generations, so consistency across the six badges comes from repeating the same **Shared Style Block** every time and only swapping in the **tier-specific block** below it. Run one generation per tier (six total), generate a few variations each, and pick whichever reads clearest at small size — these are meant to work as tiny in-app badges, not just standalone art.

---

## Shared Style Block (paste before every tier's prompt)

> Flat vector badge/patch illustration for a youth sports achievement system, bold thick black ink outlines, screen-print poster style, sticker/patch aesthetic like a varsity letterman patch or a collectible trading-card foil badge. Youth-centric, gamified, energetic streetwear look — think skate-brand graphic badges and arcade level-up icons, playful but polished, not corporate. Color palette: warm creamsicle orange and peach as the base, electric neon blue as the lead accent color, with hot pink and neon green as secondary pops, on a cream background, black linework holding it all together. Badge shape: circular or shield-shaped patch with a bold outlined border. Tier name lettered in a bold graffiti/spray-paint street-art font, multi-color letter-by-letter or gradient fill across the neon palette. Clean flat vector shading only — no photorealism, no 3D-rendered gradients, no muddy or desaturated colors, no real sports team logos or licensed brand marks, no watermarks or stray text, no fine clutter that would disappear at a small icon size. Subtle drop shadow for a peeled-sticker feel is welcome. Square image, plain or transparent background outside the badge shape.

---

## Tier-Specific Additions

Append one of these to the Shared Style Block above, per generation. Icon complexity intentionally escalates tier to tier so the full set visually reads as a progression.

**1. Rookie** — Simple, clean, entry-level badge. Central icon: a single outlined star or a minimal sneaker/ball silhouette. Mostly one accent color plus black linework — the least embellished badge in the set, signaling "just getting started."

**2. Grinder** — Central icon: a gear/cog or a clenched fist, with small motion lines or a sweat-drop accent. Two-tone coloring (one warm + one neon), slightly more detail than Rookie — conveys steady, consistent effort.

**3. Baller** — Central icon: a crown or an interlocking chain-link motif, streetwear "flexing" flair, a touch of sparkle/shine accents. Three colors from the palette in play — confident, respected-among-peers energy.

**4. All-Star** — Central icon: a bursting or shooting star with radiating sparkle trails, more dynamic movement lines than prior tiers. Brightest, boldest color combination in the set so far.

**5. Elite** — Central icon: a faceted diamond/gem, sleeker and more polished linework than the others, cooler tones emphasized (blue and pink leading). Should read as noticeably more refined/rare than All-Star.

**6. Legend** — Central icon: a flame-wrapped crown, or a lightning bolt wrapped in flame. The most embellished badge in the entire set — extra rays, foil-like highlight strokes, full multi-color palette used at once. This is the rarest, most aspirational badge; it should visually announce that before anyone reads the name.

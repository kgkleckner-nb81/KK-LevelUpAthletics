# Level Up Athletics — Skills Lab Move Guide

Your quick-hit cheat sheet for every move in the Skills Lab. Read the "How to" before you try it, and always respect the "Don't" — that's the stuff that gets people hurt.

**Ground rules for every single one of these:**
- Warm up first (some light jogging + arm circles) before going hard.
- Good form > more reps. Sloppy reps don't count toward getting better, and they're how you get hurt.
- If something hurts (not just "burns," but sharp or joint pain) — stop and tell a coach or parent. Pushing through real pain is never worth it.

---

## 💪 Strength

**Push-ups**
- Hands under shoulders, body in one straight line from head to heels.
- Squeeze your core and butt tight, lower your chest almost to the floor, elbows at about a 45° angle, then push back up.
- 🚫 Don't let your hips sag or pike up in the air — that's cheating your core and can tweak your lower back.

**Wide Push-ups**
- Same as a push-up, just set your hands out wider than shoulder-width.
- Keep that same straight-line body and control the lowering — don't just drop.
- 🚫 Don't flare your elbows all the way out to 90° — a slight bend angle protects your shoulders.

**Squats**
- Feet shoulder-width, toes turned slightly out. Sit your hips back and down like you're sitting in a chair.
- Chest up, knees tracking over your toes, go as low as you can control.
- 🚫 Don't let your knees cave inward — keep them pushed out in line with your feet the whole time.

**Jump Squats**
- Squat down like normal, then explode straight up as high as you can.
- Land soft with bent knees and reset right into the next rep.
- 🚫 Don't land stiff-legged — always land quiet and soft to protect your knees and ankles.

**Glute Bridge**
- Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat on the floor.
- Squeeze your glutes and lift your hips up until your body's a straight line from shoulders to knees.
- 🚫 Don't overarch your lower back to get higher — squeeze your butt to lift, not your spine.

**Drop Lunges**
- Step one leg back and slightly behind you, drop that back knee toward the ground, then push back up to standing.
- Alternate legs each rep.
- 🚫 Don't let your front knee cave inward or shoot way past your toes.

**Pull-Ups**
- Grab the bar just outside shoulder-width, hang with arms fully straight, then pull your chin over the bar leading with your chest.
- Lower back down under control — don't just drop.
- 🚫 Don't kip or swing wildly to "cheat" a rep up — that yanks on your shoulders. Full arm extension at the bottom, every rep.

**Dead Hang**
- Grab the bar with a firm grip, feet off the ground, shoulders relaxed but engaged, and just hang there and breathe.
- 🚫 Don't hang until your grip suddenly gives out and you drop wrong — hop off and reset the second your hands start slipping.

## 🧱 Core

**Plank**
- Forearms and toes on the ground, body in one straight line, core braced tight, eyes down at the floor.
- 🚫 Don't let your hips sag toward the floor or pike up toward the ceiling.

**Side Plank**
- Lie on your side, prop up on one forearm stacked right under your shoulder, lift your hips so your body forms a straight line.
- Stack your feet on top of each other.
- 🚫 Don't let your hips drop or rotate forward — keep everything stacked and square.

**Hollow Hold**
- Lie on your back, press your lower back flat into the floor, and lift your shoulders and legs slightly off the ground, arms reaching overhead.
- Hold that "banana" shape and breathe steady.
- 🚫 Don't let your lower back arch up off the floor — that means your legs are too low, so raise them a bit until your back stays flat.

**Dead Bugs**
- Lie on your back, arms reaching straight up, knees bent at 90°.
- Slowly lower one arm and the opposite leg toward the floor while keeping your lower back flat, then switch sides.
- 🚫 Don't let your lower back lift off the floor as you lower — that means you're going too low too soon. Only go as far as you can control.

**Bird Dog**
- On hands and knees, extend one arm and the opposite leg straight out, hips staying level, hold briefly, then switch.
- 🚫 Don't let your hips twist or rotate open — keep them square to the floor the whole time.

**Bear Crawl**
- Hands and toes on the ground, knees hovering just above the floor, crawl forward moving opposite hand and foot together.
- Keep your hips low and steady.
- 🚫 Don't let your hips pop up high in the air — keep your back flat like a tabletop.

## 🏃 Speed

**10-yard Sprint**
- Start in an athletic stance, drive your first steps low and hard, pump your arms, sprint all the way through the line.
- 🚫 Don't stand straight up right away — popping up too early kills your acceleration.

**20-yard Sprint**
- Same drive-phase start, gradually rising up into a tall sprint as you go, eyes up, arms driving front to back.
- 🚫 Don't slow down before you reach the line — always sprint through it, not to it.

**40-Yard Sprint**
- Same mechanics, just longer — drive phase into a full, tall stride, relaxed shoulders and face.
- 🚫 Don't tense up your shoulders or clench your jaw — staying relaxed at top speed is what actually makes you faster.

**Hill Sprint**
- Find a moderate hill, lean into the incline a bit, drive your knees up and pump your arms hard to the top.
- 🚫 Don't sprint back down the hill for "extra reps" — that's exactly how ankles get rolled. Walk back down.

**Flying Sprint**
- Build up your speed with a rolling start (jog into a sprint) before hitting your top speed through the marked zone.
- 🚫 Don't jump straight into an all-out sprint from a dead stop without warming up first — cold hamstrings pull easily.

## ⚡ Quickness

**Skater Jumps**
- Push off one leg, leap sideways, and land soft on the other leg with a bent knee, reaching the opposite arm across your body.
- 🚫 Don't land with a stiff, straight knee — that's how ankles and knees get tweaked.

**Lateral Shuffle**
- Athletic stance, stay low, shuffle side to side without ever crossing your feet.
- 🚫 Don't stand up tall while shuffling — staying low the whole time is what makes this drill actually work.

**Shuttle Run**
- Sprint to a line, touch it, sprint back the other way, touch again — stay low through every direction change.
- 🚫 Don't round your turns wide and lazy — plant hard and snap back the other direction.

**Carioca**
- Move sideways while crossing one foot in front, then behind, keeping your hips loose and quick.
- 🚫 Don't stare down at your feet the whole time — once you've got it, keep your head up.

**Zig-Zag Cones**
- Set cones in a zig-zag line, cut hard at each one while staying low, changing direction off your outside foot.
- 🚫 Don't run wide loops around the cones — sharp, tight cuts are the entire point of this drill.

**Box Drill**
- Four cones in a square — sprint, shuffle, backpedal, then shuffle back around, staying low at every corner.
- 🚫 Don't stand up tall at the corners — that's exactly where all your speed leaks out.

## 🚀 Jumping / Plyometrics

**Broad Jump**
- Feet shoulder-width, swing your arms back then explosively forward as you jump out as far as you can.
- Land soft with bent knees, stay balanced.
- 🚫 Don't land stiff or fall backward — stick the landing balanced with your knees bent.

**Vertical Jump**
- Quick dip down, swing your arms up hard, jump straight up as high as you can.
- 🚫 Don't skip the landing — always absorb it through bent knees instead of just crashing down straight-legged.

**Single-Leg Hops**
- Balance on one leg, hop forward or in place with small, controlled hops, landing soft on that same leg each time.
- 🚫 Don't push through these if your balance feels shaky that day — do fewer reps and build up over time.

**Lateral Hops**
- Hop side to side over a line or small object, landing soft and quiet, staying light on your feet.
- 🚫 Don't let your knee cave inward when you land — keep it stacked right over your foot.

**Squat Jump**
- Squat down, then explode straight up as high as you can, landing back down into a soft, controlled squat.
- 🚫 Don't rush the landing — control it down into the next squat instead of just collapsing.

**Box Jump**
- Face a sturdy box that's an appropriate height for you, swing your arms, and jump up landing with both feet fully on top, knees soft.
- 🚫 Don't jump onto a box too high to land on with full control — and always step back down, never jump down off the box.

**Tuck Jump**
- Jump straight up and pull both knees up toward your chest, landing soft and quiet.
- 🚫 Don't lean way forward or backward in the air — stay tall and controlled.

**Jump Rope**
- Small hops, just enough to clear the rope, elbows in close to your body, wrists doing the turning.
- 🚫 Don't jump way too high off the ground — big jumps throw off your rhythm and gas you out fast.

**Pogo Jumps**
- Quick, small, springy hops using mostly your ankles, like a pogo stick — barely bend your knees.
- 🚫 Don't turn these into big bounding jumps — keep it quick, small, and low to the ground.

## 🧭 Balance

**Single-Leg Balance**
- Stand on one leg, other knee lifted, pick a spot to focus your eyes on, and hold steady.
- 🚫 Don't lock your standing knee out stiff — keep a soft bend so you can actually adjust and balance.

**Single-Leg Reach**
- Balance on one leg, hinge forward at your hips and reach the opposite hand toward the ground, keeping your back flat, then return to standing.
- 🚫 Don't round your back to reach farther — the bend comes from your hips, not your spine.

**Heel-to-Toe Walk**
- Walk in a straight line placing the heel of one foot directly in front of the toes of the other, arms out for balance.
- 🚫 Don't rush through it — slow and controlled is what actually trains your balance.

## 🧩 Coordination

**High Knees**
- Quick jog in place or moving forward, driving your knees up to about waist height, arms pumping opposite your legs.
- 🚫 Don't lean back — stay tall and really drive your knees up instead of just taking fast tiny steps.

**Butt Kicks**
- Quick jog kicking your heels straight up toward your glutes, staying light on your feet, arms pumping.
- 🚫 Don't kick your heels out to the side — keep the motion straight up and down.

**Ladder Quick Feet**
- Run through an agility ladder (or taped squares on the ground) with fast, light steps in the assigned pattern.
- 🚫 Don't stare down at your feet the whole drill — that actually slows your reaction time. Trust your feet.

**Crossovers**
- Step one foot over and across the other while moving sideways, quick and light, staying low.
- 🚫 Don't cross so far that you lose your balance — smaller, quicker steps beat one big lunging step.

**Mountain Climbers**
- Start in a plank/push-up position, drive your knees alternately toward your chest quickly, core tight, hips level.
- 🚫 Don't let your hips bounce up and down — keep them level like a plank the entire time.

## 🧘 Mobility

**Hip Mobility**
- Flow through moves like 90/90 switches and lateral lunges, moving slow and controlled to open up your hips.
- 🚫 Don't bounce or force a stretch — ease into your range, never yank or jerk into it.

**Shoulder Mobility**
- Slow arm circles, shoulder rolls, and cross-body reaches to loosen up your shoulders before throwing, hitting, or lifting.
- 🚫 Don't do fast, jerky arm swings — shoulders respond a lot better to slow and controlled movement.

**World's Greatest Stretch**
- Step into a lunge, drop your same-side hand to the floor, then rotate your other arm up toward the ceiling, following it with your eyes. Switch sides.
- 🚫 Don't force the rotation further than feels comfortable — a little bit every day beats forcing it once.

**Deep Squat Hold**
- Sink down into the lowest comfortable squat position, chest up, and just hang out there and breathe.
- 🚫 Don't force your heels flat to the floor if they want to pop up — that improves gradually on its own, don't force it.

**Hamstring Stretch**
- Sit or stand, extend one leg out, and hinge forward from your hips (not your back) reaching toward your toes with a slight bend in the knee.
- 🚫 Don't bounce into the stretch — hold it still and let it ease out on its own.

**Thoracic Rotation**
- On hands and knees or lying on your side, rotate your upper back and reach one arm up and open toward the ceiling, eyes following your hand.
- 🚫 Don't force the twist from your lower back — the rotation should come from your upper spine, not your hips.
